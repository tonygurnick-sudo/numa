1. Annual Leave Entitlement
Policy:
All employees are entitled to a minimum of four weeks of paid annual leave per year, in accordance with the Holidays Act 2003. This leave must be taken within 12 months of entitlement, unless otherwise agreed upon by the employer and employee. Employees may request to cash out up to one week of annual leave per year, but this must be mutually agreed upon in writing.

2. Sick Leave Policy
Policy:
Employees are entitled to ten days of paid sick leave per year after the first six months of continuous employment, as per the Holidays Act 2003. Unused sick leave can be carried over up to a maximum of 20 days. Employees must notify their supervisor as soon as possible if they are unable to attend work due to illness.

3. Health and Safety Policy
Policy:
Our company is committed to providing a safe and healthy work environment for all employees, in compliance with the Health and Safety at Work Act 2015. All employees must adhere to safety protocols, report hazards, and participate in mandatory health and safety training sessions.

4. Code of Conduct
Policy:
All employees are expected to maintain a high standard of professional behavior, integrity, and respect in the workplace. This includes adhering to company policies, treating colleagues and clients with respect, and avoiding any actions that could harm the company's reputation.

5. Equal Employment Opportunity Policy
Policy:
Our company is committed to providing equal employment opportunities to all employees and applicants, in accordance with the Human Rights Act 1993. We do not discriminate based on race, gender, age, disability, sexual orientation, or any other protected characteristic. All employment decisions are based on merit and business needs.

6. Professional Development and Training Policy
Policy:
We encourage continuous professional development and will provide opportunities for training and career advancement. Employees are entitled to attend relevant training programs and workshops, subject to approval by their manager and business needs.

7. Overtime Policy
Policy:
Overtime is defined as any hours worked beyond the standard 40-hour workweek. Employees must receive prior approval from their manager to work overtime. Overtime will be compensated at a rate of 1.5 times the employee's regular hourly wage, in accordance with the Employment Relations Act 2000.

8. Unpaid Overtime Requirement (Illegal Policy)
Policy:
All employees are required to work up to 10 hours of overtime per week without additional compensation. Overtime hours are considered part of the employee's regular duties and are necessary to meet business demands.
