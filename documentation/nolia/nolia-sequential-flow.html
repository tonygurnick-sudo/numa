Nolia Platform Overview

End-to-end procurement workflow - sequential flow, build status, and context

What is Nolia?

Nolia is an AI-powered procurement management platform built for Implementing Agencies (IAs) working with Multilateral Development Banks (MDBs) such as the World Bank, Asian Development Bank, and others.

It helps IAs create compliant procurement documents, assess vendor responses, and validate evaluation reports against MDB rules - reducing the months of manual compliance work in large-scale development projects.

The Project Context

Indonesia Health System Strengthening Project

A $4 billion initiative to overhaul Indonesia's entire health system across all 38 provinces and 514 districts, upgrading over 300,000 health facilities including 500+ hospitals.

  • Client: Indonesia Ministry of Health (MOH)
  • Funders: World Bank ($1.5B), ADB ($650M), IsDB ($846M), AIIB ($1B)
  • The largest health project ever undertaken by the World Bank

1. Prepare Procurement Documents

Months 0 - 1
1
Create ToR (Terms of Reference) To Build
AI guides the user through a Q&A to generate a Terms of Reference. Covers 4 sub-types: Goods, Works, Consulting Services, and Individual Contractors. Nolia asks clarifying questions, then generates an MDB-compliant draft.
KB: Global KB (MDB policies & templates) + Project KB (project-specific context)
2
Create RFx Documents To Build
AI generates the solicitation documents sent to the market: RFQ (Request for Quotation), RFP (Request for Proposal), RFB (Request for Bid), or EoI (Expression of Interest). Same guided Q&A flow as the ToR.
KB: Global KB + Project KB
3
Review, Edit & Download Drafts To Build
User reviews the AI-generated documents, makes manual edits, and downloads final versions. Can copy/paste individual sections or take the full auto-generated draft.
Created docs go through compliance validation before release

2. Validate Procurement Documents

Months 1 - 2
4
Validate ToR (Terms of Reference) To Build
Upload the ToR and check it against MDB (Multilateral Development Bank) compliance rules. Nolia returns a validation report highlighting issues, gaps, and recommendations before submission to the MDB for approval.
KB: Global KB + Project KB
5
Validate RFx (Request for X) Documents To Build
Upload RFQ, RFP, or RFB documents and check compliance before issuing to market. Same validation flow: upload, check against Knowledge Bases, receive a detailed compliance report.
KB: Global KB + Project / Procurement Activity KB
6
MDB Signs Off on ToR & RFx External
The MDB reviews and approves the procurement documents. Can involve multiple rounds of back-and-forth. Only after sign-off can the IA (Implementing Agency) release the RFx documents to the market.
Typically 1-3 months. Outside Nolia's control.
RFx documents released to market. Vendors submit their bid responses.

3. Technical Evaluation

Months 3 - 5
7
Technical Assessment To Build
Nolia's AI reads each vendor's bid response and scores them against the evaluation criteria. Produces individual assessment reports with scores. Financial information is deliberately excluded to prevent price from biasing technical judgement.
KB: Global KB + Procurement Activity KB (contains RFx docs, rubrics, criteria)
8
Compare Vendors (Technical) To Build
Side-by-side comparison of up to 3 vendor responses. Shows scores, pros/cons, criteria-based analysis, and key differentiators. Nolia presents objective facts only - it does not recommend a vendor. Exportable as PDF.
9
IA Writes the TER (Technical Evaluation Report) External
A human at the Implementing Agency writes the formal TER, using the scores and comparison data from steps 7-8. This is a formal MDB-required document summarising the technical evaluation decisions.
Written outside Nolia, but informed by Nolia's assessment output.
10
Validate TER (Technical Evaluation Report) Built
The IA uploads their draft TER back into Nolia to check it against MDB compliance rules before submitting it. Nolia returns a validation report with issues, gaps, and recommendations.
KB: Global KB + Procurement Activity KB
11
MDB Signs Off on TER External
The MDB reviews and approves the TER. This must happen before the Combined phase can begin. Often involves multiple rounds of back-and-forth.
Typically 1-3 months. Required before proceeding to Combined evaluation.
TER approved by MDB. Financial data can now be considered.

4. Combined Evaluation

Months 5 - 9
12
Combined Assessment To Build
Integrates technical scores with financial data: pricing, life cycle costs, and financial proposals. Produces updated individual assessment reports combining both dimensions.
KB: Global KB + Procurement Activity KB
13
Compare Vendors (Combined) To Build
Same comparison flow as step 8, but now includes pricing, life cycle costs, and financial analysis alongside technical scores. This is the final comparison that informs the selection decision.
14
IA Writes the CER (Combined Evaluation Report) External
The IA writes the formal CER with the final vendor recommendation, using the combined assessment data from steps 12-13.
Written outside Nolia. This is the final recommendation document.
15
Validate CER (Combined Evaluation Report) Built
The IA uploads their draft CER into Nolia for compliance checking before MDB submission. Same validation flow as TER validation in step 10.
KB: Global KB + Procurement Activity KB
16
MDB Signs Off on CER External
The MDB reviews and approves the CER. Only after this sign-off can the IA notify vendors of the selection result and award the contract.
End of the procurement cycle. Total timeline: 5-9 months.
